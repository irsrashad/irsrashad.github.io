About / CV

Geospatial analyst and urban data scientist. 4+ years turning location data into decisions across mobility, climate, energy, and markets — from Jakarta to New York City.

NYU TANDON COMMENCEMENT · BROOKLYN NY · 2025

I'm a geospatial data analyst working at the intersection of urban systems, business strategy, and spatial data science. My work spans research, consulting, pre-sales solutioning, and product analytics — always anchored in one question: what should we do differently because of where things are?

Currently I split my time between GoTo Group (Gojek), where I design experiments evaluating routing and cartography improvements for one of Southeast Asia's largest mobility platforms, and Datakota, where I analyze and deliver urban data science projects across Indonesia. On the academic side, I volunteer as a research assistant with NYU CUSP × University of Connecticut on urban morphology and energy consumption research — see Research & Teaching.

I speak both GIS and the boardroom — technical depth with executive-level communication.

I hold an MS in Urban Data Science from NYU (Tandon / CUSP), am pursuing an MBA at Valar Institute @ Quantic, and trained as an urban planner at Bandung Institute of Technology. I've delivered work across urban science, climate, mobility, FMCG, healthcare, logistics, and real estate.
