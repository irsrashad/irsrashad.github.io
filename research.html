Volunteer Research Assistant — Urban Morphology & Energy Consumption
  • Evaluated spatial consistency of sociodemographic-based energy consumption models (Random Forest, MATLAB) across ZIP Code and Census Tract geographies in the Atlanta Metro Region using population-weighted areal interpolation — revealing systematic underestimation in 60%+ of matched tracts

Graduate Research Assistant — Transit Priority Atlas
